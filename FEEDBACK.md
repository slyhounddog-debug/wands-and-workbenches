# Feedback ledger: what / why / what next

The rule (also in CLAUDE.md > "What / why / what next"): **anything that changes the player's resources, goal, danger or power must answer three questions without guessing**:

1. **What happened?** A cue where the affected player is already looking.
2. **Why did it happen?** A cause tag: an icon, a line to the source, or a name.
3. **What should I focus on next?** One "next" emphasis (the weakness pip, the pointer, the reason icon).

Cue priority when several fire together: **danger > goal > resource loss > ambient** (`CFG.cue`). A higher cue holds lower toasts back (they queue and come out a beat apart: `UI.toast`), drops the ambient sounds (`AU.setAttn`) and dims the edge bubbles (`FA.attention`).

Every row below has an `id`. Code that fires the cue counts it with `FA.fb(id)` (`G.fb`, plain data); **`tools/featcheck` parses this table and fails if an id has no evidence**, so a new row without a cue (or a cue that stops firing) shows up as a FAIL. Add a row here for every new feature that changes resources, goal, danger or power, and exercise it in `tools/featcheck/run.js` (section "the feedback ledger").

## 4-player mode (online only)

Rows for the cues only 4P has. `tools/featcheck` runs a 4P game for these (the ones that need a room, the lobby bump and a guest dropping, are exercised there through the same code the network calls; `tools/mode4check/net.js` plays them through real rooms). In 4P NO cue says "the Wizard": it names which one (`UI.toast` rewrites it, every drawing and the HUD use the seat's name and colour).

| id | Change | WHAT (cue) | WHY (cause tag) | NEXT (focus) | Where it shows |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `refract` | A Wizard casts a pair that his attunement shapes: his TRUE FORM (exactly his two elements) or a form LED by one of his elements | the form's name over his head in the lead's colour (`VIOLET: STEAM BLAST`), the ingredients as packets, a ring in the lead element, the Builders' hit report at the Spell Portal carries his name | the lead element's packet and colour; the name says whose hands | send the pair that fits each Wizard (the Book's page shows every spell in both Wizards' hands) | Wizard half + Builder half: `M4.castCue`, `FA.landed` |
| `spell-land` | A delivered spell reaches the wilds | it lands as a pickup beside one of the Wizards (they alternate) with the usual ring and sound | the spell's own picture | either Wizard walks onto it to cast it (it casts for the nearest after `CFG.mode4.spellHold` s) | Wizard half: `WZ.incoming` |
| `spell-grab` | A Wizard walks onto a delivered spell | the drop-in telegraph plays over HIM and the cast comes out of him | which Wizard (his drop-in, his tag) | fight | Wizard half: `WZ.spellPickup` |
